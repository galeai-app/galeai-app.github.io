  [T] Diagnostics and Early Detection / AI plays a crucial role in enhancing disease diagnosis. Modern machine learning algorithms can analyze medical images, such as X-rays and MRIs, with high accuracy. This allows doctors to detect diseases like cancer, heart diseases, and neurological disorders more quickly and accurately. / Personalized Treatment / AI also contributes to the development of personalized medicine. By analyzing patient data, including genetics, lifestyle, and medical history, AI helps doctors create individualized treatment plans. This improves treatment outcomes and reduces side effects.
