  [H] Artificial Intelligence in Education: New Horizons of Learning
--- rec749142903 type=127
  [T] Artificial intelligence (AI) is fundamentally changing the approach to education, opening up new opportunities for students, teachers, and educational institutions. The integration of AI into the educational process helps make learning more personalized, efficient, and accessible.
--- rec749142904 type=106
  [T] Personalized Learning / AI enables the creation of individualized learning plans tailored to each student's needs. AI algorithms analyze students' performance, learning styles, and interests to offer the most effective methods and materials for each of them. This helps students absorb material at their own pace and according to their preferences. / Feedback and Assessment / AI systems can provide instant feedback on assignments and tests, helping students quickly address gaps in their knowledge. Teachers also gain the ability to analyze student performance more deeply, identifying each student's strengths and weaknesses. This allows for timely adjustments to the curriculum.

--- rec749142906 type=106
  [T] Virtual Tutors / AI-based virtual tutors are becoming increasingly popular. They can conduct lessons on various subjects, explain complex concepts, and answer students' questions in real time. These tutors are available 24/7, which is especially useful for self-paced learning. / Educational Process Management / AI is also used to optimize the management of educational institutions. It helps automate administrative tasks such as scheduling, attendance tracking, and resource management. This allows educational institutions to allocate their resources more efficiently and focus on improving the quality of education.
